How it works

From three PDFs to one clear table

Four steps, a few seconds each. Here is what happens after you press Compare.

  1. 01

    Upload 2–3 PDFs

    Drop your files in. They are read right in your browser with pdf.js, page by page.

  2. 02

    Index each one separately

    Every PDF is split into ~1,000-character passages and gets its own search index (BM25).

  3. 03

    Pull the best passages from every PDF

    Your question is matched against each index, so every document contributes its top passages.

  4. 04

    Get one comparison table

    Qwen 2.5 14B writes a table, key differences and a summary, citing pages, and streams it to you.

Why it works

A separate search for every document

A normal “chat with your PDFs” tool puts everything in one shared index. Ask a comparison question and the longest or most keyword-heavy file wins: its passages fill the prompt, and the others are barely heard.

This tool searches each PDF on its own and takes the same number of passages from each one, so the model always sees all sides before it compares.

FAQ

Good questions

What kind of PDFs work?

Text-based PDFs: spec sheets, brochures, policies, reports. Scanned images without a text layer can’t be read yet (there’s no OCR).

Are my files uploaded?

No. The browser extracts the text. Only the few passages matched to your question are sent to the model, and nothing is stored.

Why does an answer take 15–30 seconds?

The model runs on a single lab GPU at about 5–6 tokens per second. The answer streams in, so you can start reading straight away.

What does “Not mentioned” mean?

The document has no information for that aspect. The prompt forbids guessing or using outside knowledge, so gaps are shown honestly.

Why is the model sometimes offline?

Qwen runs on a lab machine reached through a tunnel. If that machine or the tunnel is down, the status shows “Model offline” and the app tells you clearly.
